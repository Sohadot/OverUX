01 / Thesis

Generation is getting cheap. Judgment is not.

Generative UI expands the rate and variability of interface production. OverUX treats inspection as a first-class layer rather than a downstream polish pass.

↑

Production velocity

More interface variants, more generated states and more changes can reach review faster.

≠

Evidence of fitness

Visual completion does not itself establish clarity, accessibility, trustworthy behavior or recoverability.

02 / Position

The oversight plane.

OverUX sits conceptually above implementation. It does not replace design, research, accessibility testing or standards. It organizes the decision boundary before release.

03 / OAF v0.1

Six lenses. No magic number.

The OverUX Assurance Framework is a research model for structured review. Each dimension asks a different question; none can silently compensate for another.

C1

Clarity

Can a user understand current state, available action and likely consequence?

C2

Continuity

Does the experience preserve context, state and predictable behavior across the flow?

F1

Friction

Is the effort imposed proportionate to the user's task and necessary constraints?

A1

Accessibility

Are barriers to perceiving, operating and understanding the interface identified against applicable requirements?

T1

Trust

Are consequences, reversibility, persuasion and system behavior represented without avoidable deception or ambiguity?

R1

Recovery

Can errors be prevented where reasonable, diagnosed when they occur and recovered from without disproportionate loss?

04 / Decision grammar

Uncertainty stays visible.

OverUX deliberately avoids one composite score. A release decision should preserve the reason, evidence and unresolved uncertainty behind it.

Not assessed

Evidence absent

No release inference may be made from missing review evidence.

Review

Human judgment required

A finding or uncertainty needs contextual evaluation.

Block

Release condition violated

A defined blocking requirement or critical unresolved risk is present.

Release

Preflight satisfied

Applicable checks are evidenced with no unresolved blocking finding.
